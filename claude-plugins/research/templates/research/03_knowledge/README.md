# Knowledge

Reusable knowledge extracted from papers and experiments. One concept, method, or relationship per file. Do not dump literature summaries here.

## Subdirectories

- `concepts/` — definitions and distinctions (for example "memory conflict" vs. "interference").
- `methods/` — mechanisms that can be reused, with their validity conditions.
- `relationships/` — how concepts and methods relate, conflict, or compose.

## Entry template

```markdown
# <Name>

- Type: concept | method | relationship
- Provenance: <paper bibkey + locator, experiment id, or inference>
- Validity: <conditions under which this holds>
- Confidence: high | medium | low

## Definition / mechanism

## Why it matters

## Related

- [[other-entry]]
```
